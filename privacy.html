Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

yt-factory ("the app") is a private tool operated by its owner to prepare and upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. Only the owner signs in to it.

What the app accesses

Through YouTube API Services, the app requests permission to upload videos, set thumbnails, read the channel's own video list and read the channel's own analytics. It does not access other users' accounts or data.

What is stored

  • An OAuth refresh token for the owner's channel, stored encrypted in a private cloud secrets vault.
  • Video IDs and aggregate analytics (views, watch time, retention) for the owner's own videos, stored in a private database.

The app does not collect personal information from viewers, does not sell or share data, and does not use cookies or tracking.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. Stored tokens and analytics are deleted within 30 days of revocation or on request.
